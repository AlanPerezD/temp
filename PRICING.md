## 2. Mystery-shop 5 agencies (1 hour, do it before you set your list price)

Message agencies as a dental clinic owner (WhatsApp/form) and fill `pricing-benchmark.csv`. Ask for the quote **in writing** and ask exactly:
1. Price for a 1-page / 5-page site for a clínica dental. What's included (copy, photos, WhatsApp button, Google Maps, booking form)?
2. Domain, hosting and SSL: included? **Renewal price after year 1?**
3. Monthly maintenance/changes fee? Who owns the site if I leave?
4. Delivery time. Number of revisions. Is IGV included?
5. Do they offer SEO / Google Business Profile setup? Price.

Also collect 5 freelancers (Workana/Instagram) to see the low end.

## 3. Recommended ladder (based on the book's "raise the price every 5 clients" rule)

Anchor to the KOM Pro landing price (S/ 1,450) as **list price**, then climb. You have no portfolio, so the first rungs buy you proof, not margin.

| Clients | Offer | Price |
|---|---|---|
| 1–5 | **Free** in exchange for use + feedback + video testimonial + permission to show the site | S/ 0 |
| 6–10 | 80% off | S/ 290 |
| 11–15 | 60% off | S/ 580 |
| 16–20 | 40% off | S/ 870 |
| 21–25 | 20% off | S/ 1,160 |
| 26+ | List price (raise it again if closing >40%) | S/ 1,450 |

**Plus a recurring plan on every client** (this is the real business): S/ 90–150/month for hosting, domain/SSL, small content changes, and Google Business Profile upkeep. For the free five, include 3 months and then convert them, or charge a symbolic S/ 40 so they are customers from day 1.

Ask your accountant about **IGV (18%)** and your RUC status before quoting. Decide if your prices include or exclude IGV and say so on every quote.

## 4. Positioning for dental clinics
Don't sell "a website". Sell **"más citas por WhatsApp"**: one extra patient (a limpieza/ortodoncia/implant) pays the whole site. Put that math in your sales call: `valor de 1 paciente × pacientes extra/mes` vs. `precio`.
