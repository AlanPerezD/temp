# Lead Plan: Web Agency for Small Businesses in Peru

Built from *$100M Leads* (Alex Hormozi). The book's core idea: **you get engaged leads by doing the Core Four in volume**, with an offer so good people feel stupid saying no. Everything below is that framework applied to selling websites to pequeños negocios in Peru.

> **Decisions locked in (Oct 2026):**
> - **Niche/city: dentists and dental clinics in Lima.** Solo founder, **no portfolio yet**, no audience yet, no sales team.
> - Target buyer: owner of a small/independent clinic who gets patients through **WhatsApp, Facebook, Instagram and Google Maps** and has no website or a weak one.
> - Prices come from agency benchmarks: see `PRICING.md` (one agency's public prices so far; mystery-shop 5 more before finalizing).
> - **Lead base comes from Google Maps**, cleaned and scored with the scripts in `leads/` (see `leads/README.md`).
> - Language/tone: Peruvian Spanish, informal "tú" (switch to "usted" for doctors).

---

## 0. Definitions (from the book, keep these straight)

- **Lead** = a person you can contact. **Engaged lead** = a lead who *showed interest* (replied, booked, accepted the free audit). Engaged leads are what we count.
- **Core Four** (the only four ways to advertise):

| | One-to-one | One-to-many |
|---|---|---|
| **Warm** (people who know you) | 1. Warm outreach | 2. Post free content |
| **Cold** (strangers) | 3. Cold outreach | 4. Paid ads |

- Order of attack: **Warm → Content → Cold → Paid.** Start with 1 and 2 *this week*, add 3 from week 2, leave 4 until you have a proven offer.
- Rule of 100 → Open to Goal: commit to **100 outreach actions per day** (not 100 over six weeks). Later: "work until the number is hit."

## 1. Decide the 4 things that make advertising work

Do this on day 0. Do not skip; every script depends on it.

### 1a. One niche, one city
One offer, one avatar, one platform (Level 1 of the book's roadmap). Pick one and stay there for 30 days.

**Chosen: `[NICHO]` = dentistas / clínicas dentales, `[CIUDAD]` = Lima.** One patient (limpieza, ortodoncia, implante) is worth hundreds of soles, so "the site pays for itself with 1 patient" is an easy story. Start with districts where private clinics concentrate (Miraflores, San Isidro, Surco, San Borja, La Molina, Jesús María, Lince), then widen. Avoid big clinic groups (they have in-house marketing); the cleaner already filters the obvious ones.

### 1b. The core offer (Grand Slam Offer, value equation)
Maximize dream outcome + perceived likelihood; minimize time delay + effort.

> **"Tu página web profesional con botón de WhatsApp, Google Maps y formulario de citas, lista en 7 días. Tú solo me mandas el logo y 5 fotos. Si no está lista en 7 días, no pagas."**

| Value-equation lever | What we say |
|---|---|
| Dream outcome | More clientes escribiéndote por WhatsApp, found on Google, looking professional |
| Perceived likelihood | Before/after examples, testimonials (after the first 5), guarantee |
| Time delay | 7 días |
| Effort & sacrifice | Zero. We write the copy, you send logo + photos |

Pricing: list price **S/ 1,450** (one-page clinic site) + **S/ 90–150/mes** for hosting, domain/SSL, small changes and Google Business Profile upkeep; climb to it with the "first 5 free, then 80% / 60% / 40% / 20% off" ladder. Full benchmark, sources, mystery-shopping checklist and ladder in `PRICING.md`. **The recurring monthly fee is where the agency value is**; always attach it.

### 1c. The lead magnet (the book's "big fast value")
Cold strangers don't trust you. Lead with something they'd normally pay for, delivered fast:

> **"Auditoría web GRATIS en video (3 min): te muestro exactamente por qué tu negocio pierde clientes en Google y WhatsApp, y cómo arreglarlo."**

Why this one: it's the book's type #1 (*reveal their problem*) delivered as a service. It uses their real business (personalization), takes you ~10 min each, and the fix is your core offer. Its cost is your time. If take rate is low, up the ante (e.g., include a free mockup of their homepage, "te dejo tu home diseñada, sin compromiso").

Make it easy to consume (book step 5): send as a video link **and** a 5-line text summary **and** an offer to explain by voice note.

### 1d. The CTA with reasons (book step 7)
Always a clear action + a reason to act now. Honest scarcity works best:

> "Esta semana solo tomo **5 negocios nuevos** para la auditoría gratis, porque cada una la grabo yo. ¿Te mando la tuya hoy?"

## 2. Channel 1: Warm outreach (start today)

Goal: your **first 5 clients, free** (testimonial + referral + case study in exchange). Book benchmark: 100 warm reach-outs → ~20 replies → ~4 take the free offer → ~1 pays. Do 100/day and you learn more in 10 days than from any course.

**10 steps, adapted:**
1. **List**: phone contacts, WhatsApp chats, Facebook friends, Instagram followers, LinkedIn, old email, family. Count them. That's your first 1,000.
2. **Platform**: start with where you have the most contacts (almost surely WhatsApp).
3. **Personalize** the opening (one real detail per person).
4. **Reach out to 100/day**, up to 3 attempts, one per day, until they reply.
5. **Reply with A-C-A**: Acknowledge → Compliment → Ask (steer toward business).
6. **Make the "¿conoces a alguien?" offer** (script in `scripts-es.md`). You're not selling; you're asking who they know. Most say "yo mismo" or refer someone.
7. **First 5 free**, in exchange for: use it, give feedback, leave a review if deserved.
8. Finished the list? Next platform, then restart.
9. **Start charging when referrals start**: next 5 at 80% off, then 60%, then 40%... raise price every 5 clients (the book's rule).
10. **Keep the list warm** (content) and probe with the 9-word email/WhatsApp: *"¿Sigues buscando conseguir más clientes por WhatsApp?"*

**Free-client scope control** (protect your time): 1-page site, 3 revisions, 7 days, you keep right to use their testimonial and a link in the footer.

## 3. Channel 2: Post free content (start this week, in parallel)

The audience is the compounding asset, not the posts. Volume wins: the book's author grew ~10x faster by posting ~10x more. Target: **1 short video/day minimum** on Facebook Reels, Instagram Reels and TikTok, same video cross-posted, plus daily WhatsApp Status. Facebook still matters a lot for small business owners in Peru; don't skip it.

**Content unit = Hook → Retain → Reward.**

Hook (use 2+ of: recency, relevancy, celebrity, **proximity**, conflict, unusual, ongoing). Proximity is your edge:
- "Si tienes un negocio en **Miraflores/San Juan de Lurigancho/Arequipa**, mira por qué Google no te muestra"
- "3 errores de las páginas web de las clínicas dentales en Lima"
- "Mi cliente recibía 0 mensajes. Cambié 3 cosas en su web y ahora recibe 15 al día" (once you have it)

Retain with lists, steps, stories ("5 cosas que todo negocio debe tener en Google", "Cómo crear tu ficha de Google Maps en 10 min").

Reward: actually give the answer. Give-give-give-ask: ~4–10 value posts per 1 ask. The ask: *"Comenta AUDITORÍA y te hago la tuya gratis."* (comment-to-DM works well on these platforms).

**Topic bank (steal from real life):** each audit you record = one piece of content (anonymize or get permission before showing a specific business); each client call; each before/after; mistakes you see; FAQs ("¿Cuánto cuesta una web?", "¿Necesito dominio .pe o .com?", "¿Web o solo Instagram?").

**Weekly measure:** followers, reach, growth rate, and *engaged leads from content* (DMs/comments asking for the audit).

## 4. Channel 3: Cold outreach (start in week 2, once you have 2–3 demo/free sites)

Cold = strangers don't know or trust you. Three problems, three fixes:

### Problem 1: no way to contact them → build a list
Order from easiest to most manual (the book's software → brokers → elbow grease):
1. **Google Maps / Google search** by `[NICHO] + [distrito]`. Look for listings with **no website** or a Facebook page as the "website". These are your best leads: the audit writes itself. Scrapers/exporters for Google Maps exist (search "Google Maps scraper"), or copy 1,000 manually into the tracker.
2. **Facebook and Instagram business pages** with WhatsApp-only contact, outdated posts, or a link-in-bio that goes nowhere.
3. Local directories, chamber of commerce lists, gremio/colegio profesional member lists (e.g., Colegio Odontológico if your niche is dental), Páginas Amarillas.

**This is now automated: see `leads/README.md`.** `fetch_places.py` pulls clinics by district from the Google Places API (or use an export tool), `clean_leads.py` normalizes Peruvian phones (mobile → `wa.me` link), drops closed/non-dental/institutional/duplicate rows, classifies each clinic's website (none / social / free builder / own), and scores them A/B/C. Then you curate Tier A and B by hand and add a real personal detail. Log outreach in `lead-tracker.csv` (or directly in the clean file, which has the same columns).

### Problem 2: they ignore you → personalize + big fast value
- First line proves you know them (a real detail: their rating, a post, a service they offer).
- Then deliver the lead magnet, not a pitch: *"Te grabé un video de 3 min con 3 cosas que te están costando pacientes en Google."*
- Never open with "¿quieres comprar una página web?" The book's example: that opener gets hung up on.

### Problem 3: not enough shots → volume + follow-up
- **100 touches/day** spread across WhatsApp, Instagram DM, Facebook Messenger, email, and calls.
- Contact **multiple times, multiple ways** (e.g., Day 1 WhatsApp/DM, Day 2 email, Day 3 call "para ver si te llegó el video").
- Whole list done? Wait 3–6 months and run it again; circumstances change.

**Peru-specific cautions (verify with a lawyer/contador, I'm not giving legal advice):**
- Keep it **B2B, one-to-one, personalized, with an easy opt-out** line ("si no te interesa, dímelo y no te escribo más"). Data protection law (Ley N.° 29733) and the anti-spam email law (Ley N.° 28493) apply; INDECOPI's *Gracias… no insista* registry covers telemarketing to consumers.
- WhatsApp bans numbers that blast unsolicited messages. Use **WhatsApp Business**, send manually, ramp up slowly (start ~20–30 new chats/day), and stop at once if people report you. Personalized video is what keeps you safe *and* converts.

**Cold benchmarks from the book** (your numbers will differ, track yours): phone 100 calls → ~20 pick up → ~4 engaged. Email 100 → ~30 open → ~3 engaged. Personalized DM/voice note → ~20 reply. Success = lifetime profit per customer ≥ 3× cost to get them (the portfolio example hit 10:1 to 30:1).

## 5. Channel 4: Paid ads (later, not now)

Don't start here. When you have a proven offer + testimonials (≈ month 2–3), run **Meta (Facebook/Instagram) Click-to-WhatsApp** ads for the audit offer to `[NICHO]` in `[CIUDAD]`. Per the book: spend most effort on the first 5 seconds of the ad; set the budget backwards from the customers you can handle; collect contact info on a simple page or WhatsApp.

---

## 6. Lead getters (after you have a few paying clients)

Section IV of the book: get other people to get leads *for* you.
- **Referrals / word of mouth**: ask every happy client "¿a quién más conoces con negocio?"; offer a clear reward (e.g., 1 month free hosting or S/ 150 per referred client).
- **Affiliates & partners** (best fit for a web agency): people who already serve your same small businesses and don't sell websites: **contadores, community managers, fotógrafos, diseñadores gráficos/imprentas, proveedores de POS/Yape-Plin, consultores**. Offer 15–20% commission or a revenue share. Reach them with *warm/cold outreach* too: they are a lead type.
- **Employees / freelancers**: once outreach is predictable and profitable, hire someone (even part-time) to do the 100/day. Goal: at least 3× return.

## 7. Daily operating system (Open to Goal)

Block 3–4 focused hours first thing, before anything else. Not done until:

- [ ] 100 outreach actions done (warm early on, cold + warm later)
- [ ] 1+ video posted (cross-posted) + WhatsApp Status
- [ ] All replies answered (A-C-A) and follow-ups sent
- [ ] Every contact logged in `lead-tracker.csv`
- [ ] 2 min end-of-day scorecard: outreach sent / replies / audits accepted / calls booked / sales

### One-page advertising checklist (from the book)
1. **Lead type**: customers (later: affiliates, employees)
2. **Method**: warm outreach + content (week 1), + cold outreach (week 2)
3. **Daily commitment**: 100 outreach + 1 video
4. **Offer/lead magnet**: Auditoría en video → Web en 7 días
5. **Benchmark**: 100 outreach → ~20 replies → ~4 audits accepted → ~1 paid
6. **Repeat** until you can pay someone else to do it, then make *employees* your next lead type.

| Days | Focus | Output |
|---|---|---|
| 0–1 | Decisions done (dental, Lima). Run the lead pipeline on 5 districts; mystery-shop 5 agencies for prices; build **2 demo sites** (section 10); set up WhatsApp Business + Loom/OBS | ~500 cleaned leads, price list, 2 demos |
| 1–10 | Warm outreach 100/day + 1 video/day | 1,000 contacts touched; first 5 free clients accepted |
| 7–14 | Deliver the free sites in 7 days; ask each for testimonial + video review; build the cold list (1,000 businesses) | 3–5 case studies, list ready |
| 10–30 | Cold outreach 100/day with personalized video audits; keep content daily | ~30–60 audits sent/day-equivalent, calls booked |
| 20–30 | First paying clients at 80% off, then raise every 5 sales; launch referral reward; approach first 10 partners (contadores, CMs, fotógrafos) | First revenue, referral loop |

**Success metric for 30 days:** 5 case studies + first paying clients + a known cost per lead and per client. If a number is bad, don't quit: change one thing at a time (list → message → lead magnet → offer → volume) and keep volume constant. "You did 1/42 of the work required" is the usual diagnosis.

## 9. Troubleshooting (the book's logic)

| Symptom | Likely problem | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Nobody replies | Weak hook/lead magnet, or wrong list | Rework first line, up the ante on the free value, test headline |
| Replies but no audit accepted | Hidden costs too high (effort/risk) | Ask "¿qué haría falta para que valga la pena?" and remove that friction |
| Audits done, no sales | **Sales problem, not an advertising problem** | Review call/DM script, guarantee, price anchoring |
| Works, but too slow | Not enough volume | More touches/day, more channels, then hire |

## 10. No portfolio yet: how to fix it in week 1

Strangers need proof. You have none, so manufacture it fast and honestly:

1. **2 demo sites ("concept" sites)** for a *fictional-but-realistic* clinic, clearly labeled "Demo" or "Concepto", built on a template: hero + WhatsApp button, servicios, equipo, testimonios (placeholders marked as examples), mapa, form de citas, mobile-first, fast. Never present a demo as a real client or fake testimonials.
2. **Free client #1–#5 = your portfolio.** Pick them from your warm network first (friends/family who know a dentist; the script in `scripts-es.md` A3), not from cold strangers. A friend's dentist is the fastest first yes.
3. **Before/after of the "audit"**: in the video audit, show their current Google Maps listing vs. a quick mockup of their new home page (Figma/Canva or your template with their logo and 3 photos). The mockup *is* the lead magnet when you have no case studies yet.
4. After each free site: 60-second video testimonial, screenshot of WhatsApp messages (with permission), Google Search Console/Maps stats if you can show an uplift after 30 days.
5. Replace `[CASO/DEMO]` in the scripts with the demo link until you have a real case; then switch to the real one with numbers.

**Solo-founder time budget (about 8 working hours):** 3.5 h outreach (100/day) · 1.5 h content (batch-record 5 videos one day a week) · 1 h audits (6 per day × 10 min) · 1 h replies/calls · 1 h building (free sites) . If a free site eats more than 4 h, your template is too custom: cut scope.
